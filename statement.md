## Problem Statement Bug Bounty Recon Tracker

**Problem Statement:** Cybersecurity experts and pen testers have a hard time gathering their reconnaissance information across the different targets they are evaluating. Improperly organized notes lead to work duplication and unrecognized vulnerabilities.
**Objective:** To develop a locally-running Java-based application that will make it possible to manage target domains, record found vulnerabilities, and generate reports summarizing reconnaissance data.
**Possible users:** Pen testers, security researchers, and bug bounty hunters.
**Key Features:**
1. **Target management:** Add, edit and delete target domains/IPs.
2. **Vulnerability logging:** Identify the types of vulnerabilities associated with each target.
3. **Data storage:** Use data serialization to store recon data in files.
